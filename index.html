How to hold the phone

1. Stand or sit upright.
2. Hold the phone flat in your palm, screen up, arm fully extended in front of you.
3. Keep still and relaxed for the whole test. Do not rest your arm on anything.
4. A 3-2-1 countdown starts, then recording. The first 1 s is discarded.
